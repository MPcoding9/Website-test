Room Type · Private Casita

For guests who want the finca entirely to themselves

Reached by a short stone path through the olive trees, the Casita is a fully self-contained stone house — bedroom, sitting area, ensuite bathroom, and a walled garden with its own plunge pool. A wooden gate marked "Casita" closes behind you, and from there the rest of the finca feels very far away.

Breakfast is left at your gate rather than served on the main terrace, and the pergola dining table outside is yours alone. It's the room we recommend for a honeymoon, a milestone, or simply guests who value privacy above almost everything else.

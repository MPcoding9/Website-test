Room Type · Terrace Suite

For the guest who wants the whole view, privately

The Terrace Suite occupies the corner of the main house with the best light on the property. A separate sitting area, a writing desk, and French doors that fold fully open onto your own stone terrace — big enough for a table for two, a lounger, and the outdoor tub that's become the room's signature.

This is the room for an anniversary, a proper slow week, or anyone who wants to spend as much time on the terrace as in the bed. Evenings here mean a bottle of something local, the cypress trees turning gold, and no one else in view.

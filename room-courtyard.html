Room Type · Courtyard Room

For guests who want stillness without ceremony

The Courtyard Room sits on the ground floor of the main house, opening directly onto a shaded stone terrace shared by two other rooms — private enough for a slow breakfast, sociable enough if you feel like it. Inside: thick limestone walls, a hand-finished bed, and windows angled to catch morning light without the afternoon heat.

It's the room we recommend for shorter stays, solo travellers, or anyone who wants the full Son Auba rhythm — courtyard, groves, breakfast table — without needing more space than two people actually use.

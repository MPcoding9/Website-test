The Finca

A house built for doing very little, very well

Son Auba began as a working olive estate in the 1780s, and it still is one. The old press room is now our breakfast kitchen; the drying terraces are where you'll take coffee at nine and wine at seven. We kept the thick limestone walls, the uneven floors, the quiet.

There are six rooms in total, spread across the main house and two garden casitas, so the finca never feels fuller than a good dinner party. No spa menu, no daily itinerary — just a place that holds its shape and lets you slow down inside it.

Old olive groves

Ninety-year-old trees you can walk among, and oil pressed on-site each November.

Stone terraces

Hand-laid marès stone, warm underfoot, made for slow lunches in dappled shade.

Shaded courtyards

Three interior patios cooled by fig and citrus trees — no two mornings look alike.

A local breakfast table

Bread from the village oven, our own oil and honey, eggs from the neighbouring farm.

How It Works

Three steps between here and the terrace

Booking a quiet stay should feel just as unhurried as the stay itself.

1

Choose your stay

Pick from three room types built for different kinds of quiet — a courtyard room for a short reset, the terrace suite for the full finca experience, or a private casita for total seclusion.

2

Arrive & settle in

We collect you from Palma or Manacor by request. On arrival: a glass of house wine, a walk through the groves, and a room already turned down for the evening.

3

Live at finca pace

Breakfast whenever you wake. Long lunches, longer swims, and nothing on the schedule unless you ask us to arrange it — a boat day, a vineyard visit, a massage on the terrace.
